# Boltsort store listing

**Title:** Boltsort: Unscrew & Sort

**Short description (80 characters max):**
Unscrew bolts, drop plates and sort colours into boxes. 60 free levels.

**Long description:**
Every screw on the top plate has a colour, and every box takes only its own colour. Unscrew one, and it drops into the matching box. If no box will take it, it goes to the tray. Fill a box and it clears, then starts on its next colour. Clear every plate before the tray fills up.

Boltsort has 60 levels that step up from two colours and two plates to six of each. A solver checks every level, so every puzzle can be won. Earn up to three stars per level. Using undo, a hint or an extra tray slot lowers your rating.

Every day there is a new daily level, the same for everyone on that UTC day.

Boltsort is free, MIT-licensed, and has no ads, accounts or tracking. Your progress stays on your device.
